Gapdrop Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the Gapdrop app for iPhone (“the app”), made by BlueNight Studio (“we”), collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

In short: we don’t run servers, we have no user accounts, and we don’t collect your personal information ourselves. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some information as described below.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (best score, coins, unlocked skins, daily missions) and settings (sound, haptics) are saved on your iPhone only, with a copy of your progress in the iPhone’s keychain so it comes back if you delete and reinstall the app. We never receive this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show ads: full-screen ads between some runs, and optional video ads you choose to watch to continue a run or double your coins. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and app version;
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown or tapped;
  • identifiers: the identifier for vendors, and the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking (see below);
  • diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information of the ads SDK.

Google processes this information under its own policies. See Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices for ads

  • Tracking permission: iOS asks whether the app may track you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are less personalized. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: the app asks for your consent with Google’s consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy Settings.
  • US state privacy laws: where these laws apply, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your information for targeted ads in the app: Settings → Privacy Settings.
  • Remove Ads: a one-time in-app purchase turns off full-screen ads. Optional video ads stay available, and are only shown when you choose to watch one.

In-app purchases

Purchases (Remove Ads, coin packs) are processed by Apple through the App Store. We don’t receive your payment details. The app only checks with Apple which purchases you own. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app submits your best scores to the Gapdrop leaderboard, where other players can see your Game Center nickname and score. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings.

Sharing your score

When you tap Share, the app opens the iOS share sheet with your score and a picture of your run. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

Children

Gapdrop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we don’t hold your personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Erasing your iPhone removes your game data; deleting the app alone keeps your progress for a reinstall. For information collected by Google for ads, use the choices above or Google’s My Ad Center. If you have a question or request, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
