How to play

Tap to drop the block through the gap in the moving bars. Land it in the centre for a Perfect; Perfects in a row build a combo worth extra points. The bars speed up and the gaps get narrower, split in two and start to slide.

Frequently asked questions

I bought Remove Ads on another device, or reinstalled the app.

Open Settings in the game (the gear icon) and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

I still see some ads after buying Remove Ads.

Remove Ads turns off the full-screen ads between runs. Video ads are only shown when you choose to watch one, to continue a run or double your coins, so they stay available.

My progress is gone.

Your best score, coins, skins and missions are saved on your iPhone only, and are lost when the app is deleted. Purchases can always be restored (see above).

The leaderboard says to sign in.

Sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings → Game Center, then open Ranks again.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Tracking: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Consent for personalized ads (where required): Settings in the game → Privacy Settings. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Settings in the game → Sound and Haptics.
